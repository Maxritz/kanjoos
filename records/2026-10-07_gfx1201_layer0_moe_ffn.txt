== what this PASS does and does not prove ==
PROVES
  - gfx1201 executes the Q4_K and Q6_K expert GEMMs through rocWMMA at
    16x16x16/wave32 and produces the right numbers: every element of every one
    of the 1024 gate, 1024 up, 1024 down and 512 layer-output rows was compared,
    not a stride. unwritten=0 and rows-over-tol=0 on all of them.
  - The 3.85 / 2.76 TFLOP/s figures are the measured cost of shipping real
    Q4_K/Q6_K decoders through the WMMA pipeline on this part.

DOES NOT PROVE
  - That the model prefills. Attention is not on device, the 28-layer stack is
    not on device, and no token has been generated on device. The 2379 tok/s
    figure is DERIVED from one layer and two assumptions; it is not a result.
  - That the SIMT fallback is a speed path. 222.62 + 125.96 ms per pass is ~60x
    slower than the WMMA path -- it is the C19 escape hatch, not a competitor.

== open question this run RAISES ==
  3.35 TFLOP/s is 6.9% of the 48.7 TFLOP/s RDNA4 dense-FP16 class peak, well
  below the previously MEASURED 19.12 TFLOP/s (39%) microbenchmark. Candidate
  explanations, none yet tested: (a) grid.y=17 with 64-thread blocks underfills
  the 32 CUs; (b) the kernel is weight-path bound, not MAC bound -- it decodes
  Q4_K as it consumes it, and the per-element decode in the WMMA path costs what
  the SIMT path costs, so the MACs are not the bottleneck at all; (c) 4 experts
  means the M dimension is thin (512 tokens / 4 experts = 128 rows per expert).
  This is recorded as an open question, NOT explained away.
